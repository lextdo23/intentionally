Your privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026

What we keep, and why

Without an account: nothing, unless you turn on reminders (we keep your phone's notification address and the times you chose), share a card with everyone (its words and the name you pick), or join as a tester (what you tap, under a random code, never your words).

With an account: your email (to send sign-in codes), your username, the public half of your Face ID key (your face never leaves your phone), and a backup of your days, including anything you've typed, so a lost or new phone doesn't lose them. Your photos are backed up too, made smaller and with nothing but the picture (no location, time or phone details); only you can see them, and you can turn this off in Settings, which deletes the copies. Mood and sleep can be sensitive: they're only used to show them back to you.

If you add friends: the name, "This season" line and picture you choose, who your friends are, requests you send or get, people you block, and that you said you're 18 or over. Only your friends see your name, line and picture, plus your friends count unless you hide it. Someone who knows you and a friend of yours can see that you're friends with each other ("Friends with …" on a profile, request or search result); turning off "Show my friends" hides your friends count and leaves you out of that. If your picture is a photo, a small copy (with nothing but the picture: no location, time or phone details) is kept with your profile so your friends can see it, and so can someone you ask to be friends; everyone else sees a little character instead. Picking a little character deletes the copy. Someone who looks up your exact username sees only your name and picture (a little character, never your photo), and which of their own friends are your friends too. Your email is never shown to anyone.

If you share a moment: only what you pick: that day's photo (made smaller, with nothing but the picture: no location, time or phone details), with that day's card behind it (its words and pillar) unless you choose "Leave it off", or the card on its own (its words and pillar), or your week's card from the weekly recap (the picture just as you see it, with moods and photos only if those are switched on), your caption, and how the day felt only if you turn that on (a mood word and feelings, never numbers). Your friends see it, and who said "I see you". It stays until you remove it.

If you turn on friend notifications: your phone's notification address and its two notification keys (so only your phone can read them), your time zone (so nothing comes at night), when you were last notified, and which new moments are still waiting to be told. Each notification says only a friend's name (or username) and that they shared, sealed so the phone maker's notification service can't read it. Turning them off, signing out or deleting your account removes all of it.

If you report something: we keep your report (the reason, any words you add, and who sent it) and a copy of what you reported (for a profile, including its photo), so we can look at it even if it's deleted. The person is never told who reported them. We may remove a moment or pause an account's sharing. Reports are deleted 90 days after we've looked at them.

If you share with your doctor: the summary is made on your phone, and only goes where you send it (by email, AirDrop, Messages or anything else on your phone). We never see it or keep a copy. It holds only the parts you switch on, plus your name and things to talk about if you add them. Your name and which parts you picked are remembered on your phone (and in your backup, if you have an account) for next time.

The services we use to run Daydeck may store or handle your information outside Australia, including in the United States and Japan. They only use it to run Daydeck.

Your choices

  • Get a copy: Settings → Save a copy.
  • Moments: remove any moment you shared (tap ••• on it), and hide a friend's moments from your feed.
  • Friends: turn off friend requests, hide your friends (your count, and you as a friend in common), remove or block anyone (they aren't told). Settings → Privacy.
  • Friend notifications: off unless you turn them on; turn them off any time in Settings → Nudges, "When friends share".
  • Delete your account and backup: Settings → Your account → Delete account. It's gone straight away, friends and moments included.
  • Delete what's on this phone: Settings → Delete everything.
