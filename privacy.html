Your privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026

What we keep, and why

Without an account: nothing, unless you turn on reminders (we keep your phone's notification address and the times you chose), share a card with everyone (its words and the name you pick), or join as a tester (what you tap, under a random code, never your words).

With an account: your email (to send sign-in codes), your username, the public half of your Face ID key (your face never leaves your phone), and a backup of your days, including anything you've typed, so a lost or new phone doesn't lose them. Mood and sleep can be sensitive: they're only used to show them back to you.

If you add friends: the name, "This season" line and picture you choose, who your friends are, requests you send or get, people you block, and that you said you're 18 or over. Only your friends see your name, line and picture, plus your friends count unless you hide it. Someone who looks up your exact username sees only your name and picture. Your email is never shown to anyone.

The services we use to run Daydeck may store or handle your information outside Australia, including in the United States and Japan. They only use it to run Daydeck.

Your choices

  • Get a copy: Settings → Save a copy.
  • Friends: turn off friend requests, hide your friends count, remove or block anyone (they aren't told). Settings → Privacy.
  • Delete your account and backup: Settings → Your account → Delete account. It's gone straight away, friends included.
  • Delete what's on this phone: Settings → Delete everything.

Questions

Email hello@daydeck.day and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if it's a big change.

Daydeck is made in Australia, for adults. It isn't therapy or medical care. Need to talk to someone now? Lifeline: 13 11 14.
