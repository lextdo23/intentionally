Friends guidelines

Last updated 21 November 2026

Friends on Daydeck is a small, quiet place to be seen and supported by people you know. A few things keep it that way.

  • Share your own days.
  • Be kind.
  • Keep it safe.
  • Be yourself.

Share your own days

Your photos, your cards, your words. Ask before sharing a photo with someone else in it.

Never share someone else's private details, like where they live, their phone number, or where they'll be.

Be kind

No bullying, put-downs, hate or threats, including about anyone's body, background, beliefs, gender or sexuality.

No pressure. Nobody has to share, do more, or keep up. Daydeck is never a competition.

Keep it safe

No sexual content or nudity, and nothing violent or gory.

Nothing that shows or encourages self-harm, suicide or disordered eating. That includes weights, calories, "before and after" photos and extreme diets or exercise.

No drugs, and nothing that makes getting drunk look like the goal.

All of this goes for your profile picture and name too.

If you're struggling, you don't have to keep it to yourself. Lifeline is there any time: call 13 11 14 or text 0477 13 11 14. In danger right now, call 000.

If a friend's moment worries you, reach out to them, and report it with "They might not be safe". Daydeck will look at it, and they won't know it was you.

Be yourself

One account, as you. Don't pretend to be someone else, or Daydeck.

No spam, ads or selling. Friends is for friends.

Friends is for adults: you need to be 18 or over.

If something's not right

You're always in control. From the ••• on a friend's moment or profile you can hide their moments, remove them, or block them. They aren't told.

Report anything that breaks these guidelines. A real person at Daydeck looks at every report. We may remove a moment, or pause an account's sharing if it keeps happening. The person reported is never told who reported them.

Think we got something wrong? Email hello@daydeck.day.
