## What this app is (signed off by alex, 2026-11-03). Check every idea against this first.
Full one-pager: https://claude.ai/artifact/3YMBhpwDit9UNVWP2V2qHY

- **Purpose:** live with intention. Choose the few parts of life that matter, give them time, and see where your time really goes, a week at a time.
- **Built for:** people aged 18 to 30, burnt out or low (not in crisis), in Australia first: young workers early in their careers (Mia, 24) and university students (Josh, 19).
- **The rule:** a feature or fix goes in only if it helps them, or costs them nothing (no extra steps, nothing extra on screen). Others are welcome, but we don't design around them.
- **The core loop (time leads; everything else supports it):**
  1. Choose: 3 to 5 pillars, 2 of them focus, chosen on day one (the app helps people choose).
  2. Give time: tap a planet to time it, or log "I gave time" afterwards with a rough length. Both count the same.
  3. See: tap the sun any time for "Your week" (each planet's time this week). No numbers on the sky itself (alex, 2026-11-03). Lead with what happened, never gaps, comparisons or targets.
  4. Reflect: the weekly look back (the weekly recap) is always on Sunday (alex 2026-11-22: kept fixed, no choosing a day): ready once Sunday is ended, or any day after for last week, and waits on home until the person is ready.
- **Supporting features, one job each:** the sky (shows life at a glance), the weekly look back, catching thoughts (where your mind goes, beside where your time goes), sorting thoughts, words of affirmation (kind, believable lines tied to their pillars; for kind, worry and want thoughts; never for heavy thoughts), seasons, safety.
- **Not:** a productivity tracker, a journal first, or therapy.

## Tech
- The app is Daydeck, served at **https://daydeck.day** (alex, 2026-11-20): GitHub Pages with a custom domain (DNS on Cloudflare). Daydeck lives at the front of the repo; the old Intentionally app is retired and removed. `v2/` only forwards old links and icons on to the front page, keeping `?tester`.
- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript modules. No build step, no frameworks, no dependencies. Open `index.html` through any static server.
- All data lives on the phone: `localStorage` under the key `daydeck` (see `js/store.js`), photos in IndexedDB (`js/photos.js`). Bump `SCHEMA` and migrate when the shape changes.
- **Photos keep nothing but the picture** (alex 2026-11-20): no location, time, phone or camera details, and no note of when a photo was taken or whether it came from the camera or the library. Every photo coming in or going out (camera, library, Restore, Save a copy, Save my photos, share, and later a profile photo) goes through `clean()` in `js/photos.js`. The app never asks for access to the photo library; it uses the phone's own picker, which hands over only the one photo chosen. `node tests/photo-clean.test.cjs` checks it.
- **Opening and coming back (alex 2026-11-21, "like other social apps"):** a fresh open shows the splash (the logo, the app icon's eclipse, on plain black, no glow; new look alex 2026-11-23; a whole sun inside the moon with a fine light rim, alex 2026-11-24, DESIGN.md 18b; styled inside `index.html` so it's there from the first moment) while home gets ready behind it (`body.booting`), then it fades and home rises in: at least 0.8s, at most about 1.5s even offline. Sheets, tips and invites wait for it (`booted` in `js/main.js`). The iPhone's own launch pictures (`icons/launch-*.png`, linked in `index.html`) are drawn from the same splash by `node tools/make-launch.cjs`; rerun it whenever the splash changes. Coming back shows Daydeck just as it was left: no splash, no reload, and home only redraws if what it shows has moved on (`lookNow()`: a new day, the greeting, or which main button). An update found while open waits for the next fresh open, or a return after 30+ minutes away (then behind the splash). `node tests/launch.test.cjs` checks it.
- **Run all the tests with `tools/test-all.sh`** (about 3 minutes: six at a time, then any failure retried on its own). While working on one screen, run only that screen's tests; the full run once before saving. Tests guard what can't be seen by looking: people's data, privacy, safety, accounts, backups and the sums behind the charts. Look and layout are checked by eye against DESIGN.md, not with new tests (alex 2026-11-24).
- `sw.js` caches the app for offline use. **Bump `VERSION` and update `FILES` whenever any file changes or is added.**
- iOS 26 lays a home-screen app out one status bar short (a strip at the bottom) unless the page is taller than that; `html` gets a `min-height` for home-screen apps in `css/app.css`. Keep it.
- Every page (`index.html`, `testers.html` and the `v2/` forwarding page) carries a Content-Security-Policy meta tag (safety net): only the app's own files and `intentionally-push.lextdo.workers.dev`. No inline `<script>` and no outside scripts; a new outside service must be added to `connect-src` in `index.html` and `testers.html`. `node tests/csp.test.cjs` checks every screen.
- Time always comes from `now()` in `js/clock.js`, never `Date.now()` directly in app logic, so test mode's time travel works. (The sky's orbit phase uses the real clock on purpose.)

## Design rules (agreed with alex; don't drift from these)
- **The new look's rules live in `DESIGN.md`** (alex 2026-11-24): numbered rules plus the list of screens still to rework. Check every screen against all of them before it's called done, show alex what it breaks first, and mark it off there.
- Feeling: wonder, grown-up, calm. No mascots, confetti, badges, streaks or targets. Bold, saturated colour is allowed (alex 2026-11-23: the palette is being redone away from the "AI" look, toward plain black or white chrome and one owned colour). Exception (alex 2026-11-13): cute characters are allowed as ART on an unlockable card style only; they never talk, send messages or react to what the person does or misses. Exception (alex 2026-11-20): default profile pictures are little living things (head-and-shoulders silhouettes: flower, smile, frog, cat, bunny, bear, chick, sprout), one tint on a darker shade of itself; pictures only, same rules.
- Palette (new look, alex 2026-11-23): plain black `#0a0a0b` everywhere (no gradient), ink `#efede9`, marigold `#ffa51f` (`--sun`) as the one owned colour: the Daydeck card back, the main button (always marigold, whatever card look is in use: it's what draws the eye to press it), the recap line, the logo. White for plain facts (the level row). Gold `#e3c486` stays for "Got it done" for now (alex: change it only if testers get confused). Pillar tints `#d9b3a6 #cfc59b #a9c4b5 #a9b9d6 #c4b4c9`. No glow anywhere except the sun in check-ins and the card draw, and the light behind today's card while it waits (DESIGN.md rule 37) (no liquid edge: removed 2026-11-25 because it made Daydeck lag; DESIGN.md 37b). Designs: the "Daydeck · Palette options" canvas.
- Font: system stack (SF Pro on Apple). Apple's standard text sizes, never scaled with the screen: large title 34, titles 28 / 22 / 20, body and buttons 17, smaller 15, notes 13, tiny labels 12 / 11 (never below 11). Only the sky scales to fill the screen.
- No decorative stars. Every star is a thought the person wrote. Kind thought = 4 points, worry = 6, want = 8. Never colour alone.
- Planets: still, set evenly around the sun (focus pillars either side). Flat, lit from the sun with a crisp shadow on the far side; surface marks, craters and bumps rotate on a sphere (all flat, same colour, light on the sun side). No orbit lines. Each name is a level ring round its planet (name twice, 13px weight 500), a lap every 20s, turning so names slide in from the left; near side readable, far side faint and mirrored behind the planet.
- Sun: the warm glow (alex 2026-11-24: rising from the horizon at the bottom, still, with fine grain so it doesn't look AI-made; DESIGN.md 9b) now shows only in check-ins and the card draw, where it tints with how the day felt (alex 2026-11-23; the draw kept it 2026-11-24 so the morning stays warm). On Today it's gone. Fewer colours: only focus pillars keep their tint.
- Today (new look, alex 2026-11-23), top to bottom: the drawn daydeck wordmark (`js/wordmark.js`, 36 points tall) with Cards (a fanned-deck icon) and Your photos in the corners (Cards is a dashed ring until level 5; Settings is on You); the level row, full width and white; Your days (words left, the week's mood line right) with the crumbs button beside it; the recap line (marigold, to catch the eye); then today's card, about 14% bigger, centred in the space left. No greeting or narration: the card and the button say what's next. The greeting's words only show as a short hint in the first week; nothing after "I did it" (alex 2026-11-26: "Card's done. Next, end your day tonight." was unnecessary and pushed the card). **"I did it" on home** (alex 2026-11-26, `askFelt` in `js/draw.js`): nothing on the page may jump or overlap. The level row and Your days stay; the boxes line and the recap line fade out (no re-layout) while the card shrinks up to sit 24 under Your days, with the question 24 under the card (question and feelings close together, 24 before Done, Not now right under it). On Done the card grows home with no mid-way hitch, the camera fades as the feeling word comes in, and as it lands "Add a photo" / "Not done yet", the level row and the bottom pill come back together; the two lines fade back once the card has grown past them. The space under a to-do card is kept at its done size, so the card never shrinks as it lands, and the redraw after changes nothing visible. The boxes and recap lines sit as a pair (each a full 44-point tap area, words at the inner edges). A new level has its own moment (alex 2026-11-24, DESIGN.md rule 38, `js/levelup.js`, `node tests/levelup.test.cjs`): the eclipse opens with "Level 5", then a card for what opened with "Open …" / "Later" (in place of the pointing tip); then the bar ticks over. Tapping the wordmark five times switches test mode (it used to be the greeting). No Lifeline line on Today: it stays in every check-in, the kept card, Your days, Friends and sign-up, so it's one step away. The morning hand of three has none (alex 2026-11-25, to clear the bottom; the sleep check-in just before it has one).
- **Not open yet** (alex 2026-11-25, "Daydeck · Greyed-out unlocks" canvas): things a level opens that have a spot of their own (Friends in the pill, Cards in the top corner) show from day one in that spot as an empty dashed ring (6 on, 4.5 off, 2px, 60% ink) with a muted icon and no glass, so they read as a place waiting to be filled and home never rearranges. No padlocks, no level numbers on the button. Tapping one shows a small off-white bubble beside it ("Friends opens at level 3. Start and end your days to get there." and "See your levels ›"; never points or days to go), which fades after 3.5s or on any tap (`notYet` in `js/path.js`). Swiping never lands on a tab that isn't open (it springs back). Before Friends opens, You keeps Today's action in the pill (off-white) so the pill never changes shape. Things that live inside a screen (Your days row, Make your own card) still just appear at their level. Tester log `locked_tap` (which one); testers page under levels.
- Buttons you can press look pressable (alex 2026-11-23): round buttons and the Your days row are the same smoky glass as the pill, and buttons give a little (shrink and brighten) when touched.
- Thoughts never pass behind the sun or a planet; sorted thoughts circle their pillar.
- Stars roam the whole open sky on slow Lissajous laps (6 to 10 minutes), starting from where they landed.
- Running a timer zooms the sky into that planet (only the planet and the passing sun remain), then the time fades in under it.
- Sorting (`js/sort.js`): one card at a time, in the sky or on a page (planets in a row above a stack of cards). Drag the card up onto a planet, or the sun ("Just me"), to sort; swipe left to let go, right for "not sure yet". No labels on screen: the words fade in behind the card while swiping. A one-line tip shows only on the first few sorts. The round × at the top right always stops sorting. Letting go sinks down and crumbles away, with a line of relief (worries: "That was a moment, not the truth."; others: "You felt it. You don't have to keep it.").
- Thoughts are always shown in quotation marks.
- One focal point per card; everything else steps down. A label is never smaller than what it labels.
- **No keys or legends on charts** (alex 2026-11-22): if a chart needs a key to be understood, it's too confusing. Every part explains itself: words next to marks ("Hard · 5 of 7"), a short line under anything unusual, one meaning per colour.
- Mockups (alex 2026-11-15): alex's phone is an iPhone 15 Pro Max, 430 x 932 points (safe areas 59 top, 34 bottom). Every phone mockup is drawn in that frame at 1:1 with the app's real sizes; start from `/mnt/project-files/phone-frame/phone-frame.html`. Never draw in a smaller phone, or cards look bigger than they will on the phone.
- The Daydeck card (alex 2026-11-23, "Marigold slab"): the back is solid marigold with the eclipse cut into it in black and the day's date in its corner (Daydeck is a deck of days); the face stays dark with a fine white edge; when done, one shine sweeps across. While the card waits to be done, warm light shows from behind it (alex 2026-11-24, "eclipse corona": `.glow` between the faces in `js/draw.js`, moving with the sway, from the left and pale in the morning to the right and deeper orange in the evening;). "Save for later" is gone from the draw (alex 2026-11-24; cards saved before still come back first). A slower, gentler sway (5 degrees). Card looks (like Chrome) bring their own material. Today's card on home has a thick edge (alex 2026-11-23, option B on the "Daydeck · Card thickness" canvas), now marigold card stock: 3 points thick, lit from above, made of thin layers between the two faces (`EDGE` in `js/draw.js`, `.home-card .edge` in `css/app.css`; the faces' 1.5px offset is half of `EDGE`).
- One 20px left edge; spacing 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 40. Corners: sheet 26, card 16, button 14, field 12, chip 10. Buttons that are only an icon are round.
- Tap targets at least 44px. Respect reduce-motion (stop all motion) and nothing flashes.
- Voice: comforting at hard moments (worries, reflection, repeats), brief and steady everywhere else.
- Features are revealed slowly and we say so up front.
- Not therapy: crisis support (Australian lines) must always be reachable.
- Tester mode (`js/tester.js`, dashboard `testers.html`, storage in `push/worker.js`): beta testers opt in and the app sends only choices and numbers, never typed words. **Every new feature must report what people do with `log("name", value)`** (cards drawn/passed/done/thumbs, setup steps, pillars and mindsets, levels and packs), and add its section to `testers.html` (cards, setup, pillars and levels already have "not built yet" placeholders). A new KIND of data needs a line in `SHARED` and a `CONSENT` bump so testers are asked again. **Staying in, and the one-time invite (alex 2026-11-25):** tester mode lives only on the phone (never in the account backup, so tester data stays anonymous); phones were quietly dropping out when reset, so "Start again" now keeps it (`store.reset({ keepTester: true })`), while Delete everything still stops it and deletes what was shared. Everyone is asked once, "Help shape Daydeck" (`inviteDue()` in `js/tester.js`, shown by `inviteWhenFree` in `js/main.js`): people already using Daydeck on their next open, before any other message, once the screen is clear; new people now in setup (alex 2026-11-26, "Help improve Daydeck"); anyone who left setup early still after their second evening check-in. Never a phone already testing, someone who pressed Stop, or a test account. Not now means never again (`seen.testInvite`, which travels with the backup); Settings keeps "Help test Daydeck". Tester data was wiped on 2026-11-25 to start fresh with the new look. `node tests/tester-invite.test.cjs`.
- **Usage tab on the tester page** (alex 2026-11-26, `js/testers-usage.js`, `node tests/usage.test.cjs`): the technical side, built from the records tester mode already sends, grouped into visits (an open until leaving; back within a minute is the same visit): each tester's days on Daydeck, days opened (stickiness), visits a day, a usual visit, time a day, usual card time and last open; coming back 1, 2, 7, 14 and 30 days after starting; visits a day and when they start, time between visits, from a notification; visit lengths; what each visit did (`area()` names the part of the app for each log; Lifeline taps are left out so they're never tied to anything else); when the card gets done (part of the day, how long after keeping it, same visit or not); each tester's last 15 visits; error-free visits and app versions. The app logs `close` (seconds the app was open, up to 4 hours) when it's left (`left()` in `js/tester.js`), a new kind of data, so `SHARED` gained it and `CONSENT` went to 3: testers are asked again before it's sent. A new log goes into `area()` if it's a new part of the app.
- The notifications service (`push/worker.js`) only answers the app's own addresses (`APPS`: daydeck.day, plus the old GitHub address during the move).
- Weekly recap notifications (alex 2026-11-22, `recapTimes` in `js/notify.js`, words in `sw.js`): Sunday "Weekly recap tonight" / "End your day to see how your week went." at the evening nudge time (7pm if evenings are off), skipped once the day is ended; Monday "Weekly recap ready" / "A look back at Monday to Sunday, when you've got a minute." at the morning nudge time (8am if mornings are off), only while the recap still waits (tapping it opens the recap). Only from day 7 of use; always on, with no switch, for everyone whose phone allows notifications (alex 2026-11-26); never more than these two a week. On the same minute as the usual nudge it replaces it (one ping). Home says "Your weekly recap is ready". `node tests/recap-nudge.test.cjs` checks it.
- **Notifications: Nudges and Social** (alex 2026-11-26, thread "Split notifications into nudges and social", plan https://claude.ai/artifact/3cQLECmpY7Xj4JEQY5V2Xm). Settings has two groups, no paragraphs (a few words under a row only where its name isn't enough, like other apps): **Nudges** (Start your day and End your day with times, Card reminders "If your card isn't done", Wind-down while it runs) and **Social** (one switch, Friends, "Requests and new moments", once Friends is open and ticked 18+). The weekly recap reminders have no switch: always on. **Everything starts on** and people switch off what they don't want (`defaults()` in `js/notify.js`); people who already had Daydeck keep the choices saved on their phone. Nothing arrives until the phone allows notifications (Apple asks once; the setup step asks, see the sign-up thread), so while it doesn't, Nudges starts with "Notifications are off for Daydeck" and why, with Turn on when it can ask (tester log `nudge_allow` yes/no). **Who has what on:** the tester page's "Who has notifications on?" (Check-ins tab) shows one row per tester code: phone allows (yes/no, never whether Daydeck is on the Home Screen), morning and evening times, card reminders, Friends (`switches()` in `js/tester.js`, kept in the testers table's `nudges`). Testers only, never by name or username (alex chose this over a list of every account, which would break "never read").
- **"Turn on notifications?" card** (alex 2026-11-26, mockup https://claude.ai/artifact/HoeFWq6Nk5NnWhFtTU5eVD, `js/notif-card.js`): people already using Daydeck (started before today; new people are asked in setup) see it once, when home is quiet and nothing else was asked that open. Three kinds: never asked ("Turn on notifications?", Turn on brings up Apple's Allow and switches everything on), said no to Apple before ("Notifications are off for Daydeck" and where to switch them on in the phone's Settings, Okay), or allowed with some switches off ("Turn on the rest?", just those listed, already on). Not now means never again (`seen.notifCard`). Never for test accounts or before Daydeck is on the Home Screen. Tester logs `notif_card` (ask / denied / rest), `notif_card_on` (allowed / not / okay / later / which ones turned on), shown under "Who has notifications on?". `node tests/notif-card.test.cjs`.
- Notifications (`js/notify.js`, `push/`) are a FIRST VERSION, NOT FINAL: built as first drafted at alex's request; words and design get revisited. The service only knows times, never words; a thought never goes on the lock screen. The one exception is friend notifications (see Social), which carry a friend's name, sealed for that phone.
